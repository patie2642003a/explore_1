Suggested Itineraries

3-Day Gorilla Trekking Adventure 🦍

  • Day 1: Arrive in Entebbe → Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest
  • Day 2: Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi → Relax at a local lodge
  • Day 3: Explore Lake Bunyonyi → Return to Kampala or Entebbe

7-Day Safari & Cultural Tour 🐘

  • Day 1: Arrive in Entebbe → City tour of Kampala
  • Day 2-3: Safari in Murchison Falls National Park
  • Day 4: Visit Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary → Transfer to Fort Portal
  • Day 5-6: Chimpanzee tracking in Kibale Forest & Queen Elizabeth NP Safari
  • Day 7: Return to Entebbe → Departure

5-Day Adventure & Culture Trip 🚀

  • Day 1: Explore Jinja → Whitewater rafting on the Nile
  • Day 2: Sipi Falls hike & coffee tour
  • Day 3: Drive to Mbale → Mount Elgon exploration
  • Day 4: Cultural experience with the Karamojong community
  • Day 5: Return to Kampala → Ndere Cultural Centre show

Top Attractions in Uganda

  • Bwindi Impenetrable Forest: Home to the endangered mountain gorillas.
  • Murchison Falls National Park: Witness the mighty Nile river squeeze through a narrow gorge.
  • Queen Elizabeth National Park: Famous for tree-climbing lions and diverse wildlife.
  • Lake Bunyonyi: A serene lake surrounded by lush terraced hills.
  • Jinja & the Source of the Nile: Perfect for adrenaline activities like rafting and bungee jumping.

Travel Tips for Planning Your Trip

  • Best Time to Visit: June to September & December to February (Dry seasons).
  • Health& Safety: Get vaccinated for yellow fever and carry malaria prevention medication.
  • Transportation: Consider hiring a 4x4 vehicle for safaris and remote areas.
  • Local Currency: Ugandan Shilling (UGX) — carry cash for rural areas.
  • Respect Local Culture: Dress modestly, ask permission before taking photos, and learn a few Luganda phrases to connect with locals!
