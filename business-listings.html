Discover Uganda’s Vibrant Local Businesses

Uganda is home to a diverse array of local businesses that reflect the country’s rich culture, craftsmanship, and entrepreneurial spirit. From bustling markets to cozy cafés and luxurious lodges, supporting local businesses helps preserve traditions and boost the community. Whether you’re seeking handcrafted souvenirs or local delicacies, Uganda has it all!

Business Categories

  • Hotels & Lodges: Find comfortable stays, from luxury resorts to budget-friendly guesthouses.
  • Restaurants & Cafés: Taste Uganda’s vibrant cuisine — try dishes like matoke, luwombo, and rolex!
  • Craft Shops & Souvenirs: Buy handwoven baskets, wood carvings, and beaded jewelry made by local artisans.
  • Tour Operators & Guides: Connect with local tour companies for guided safaris, gorilla trekking, and city tours.
  • Transportation Services: Use local boda-bodas, taxis, or car rentals to explore the country easily.
  • Markets & Street Vendors: Explore vibrant markets like Owino Market in Kampala for fresh produce, fabrics, and more.

Why Support Local Businesses?

By choosing local businesses, you contribute directly to Uganda’s economy and help communities thrive. Many businesses also play a vital role in preserving Ugandan culture by keeping traditional crafts, recipes, and storytelling alive. Supporting small businesses ensures a more authentic and sustainable travel experience.

Tips for Shopping & Dining in Uganda

  • Bargaining is Common: Especially in markets, bargaining is expected — but do it respectfully!
  • Try Local Dishes: Don’t miss out on local favorites like chapati wraps (rolex) and grilled tilapia.
  • Carry Cash: While larger businesses accept cards, many smaller vendors only take cash (Ugandan Shillings).
  • Ask for Recommendations: Locals are incredibly friendly and often eager to recommend the best places to shop or eat.
  • Eco-Friendly Choices: Buy from businesses that use sustainable practices and avoid single-use plastics.
